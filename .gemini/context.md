# Flight Finder: implemented architecture and project context

Last updated: 2026-10-09. Start here or at [PROJECT_GUIDE.md](../PROJECT_GUIDE.md).

## Product and price evidence

This local browser app searches domestic Indian flight listings and helps verify the amount payable after coupons and checkout charges. Global oil, currency and disruption context remains relevant to Indian fares. The user enters passenger details and chooses a payment method on the provider page. The app does not purchase tickets.

A **listed fare** is a price read from search results; fees and coupon eligibility remain pending. A **verified checkout total** is an observed INR payment-page total for the selected itinerary/payment choice. Future cashback and speculative card points never reduce this number. Verified results rank first, with listings separately labelled; that does not prove a verified result is cheaper than every unverified offer online.

Live checks returned listings from Google Flights, EaseMyTrip and Cleartrip, including confirmed three-adult business criteria on Google. MakeMyTrip has an adapter but returned no usable listings in the current check. Additional saved websites are manual links. Universal coverage remains unfinished. Ambiguous group-price units are rejected. International airport pairs are currently rejected; broader baggage, cabin and fare-family coverage needs further validation.

## Request and data flow

1. React sends exact airports/date, adults, cabin, departure-time/stops preferences, providers and constraints over /ws. TaskCompiler validates these, budget, return legs and concurrency, then saves the search. Explicit ISO-date/city or airport queries parse locally; optional Gemini intake is validated before execution. No upfront bank/card ownership question is required.
2. Dispatcher tries the pinned Fli public reader for exact-date Google searches without proxies, otherwise opens criteria-specific provider browsers. Failure falls back to the browser. Parsers read real cards; validation rejects invalid money, route mismatches, preference violations, nearby-airport substitutions and ambiguous multi-adult units. MakeMyTrip containers holding multiple flight cards are rejected. At most twelve cheapest valid quotes per provider/leg are stored/emitted.
3. Integrity checks match directed route/date/adults/cabin/currency/freshness and compare the same itinerary across websites, including Google Flights when available. Nearby airports cannot silently replace the selected airport. Corroborated rows enter history; uncorroborated rows remain visible but cannot teach the brain; flagged rows are quarantined and hidden by default. Two unexplained conflicting sources are both held. Google itself can be flagged.
4. Search browsers close after extraction. Clicking Open booking re-searches and matches the selected itinerary before handing off a browser for fifteen minutes. At most three booking sessions are retained. Google provides a comparison link. Passenger details and payment choice are supplied by the user.
5. **Verify total & coupons** requires a payment-page total, itinerary/date/time, adult/cabin evidence and a selected payment method. Eligible discovered/user-supplied codes are tested individually. Bank and special card offers require a matching selected bank/product; unconfirmed account/traveller/network conditions stay conditional. Coupon radios cannot prove payment selection. Acceptance plus a stable reduction is required; removal must restore baseline, and the winner is reapplied and confirmed. Payment method and itinerary are rechecked after trials. Conflicting payment/sidebar totals are rejected.
6. Only that selected result becomes verification=checkout, with actual total, coupon, timestamp and fee breakdown. Other results remain listed. Changing payment, baggage or fare requires reverification. Ambiguous layouts produce errors instead of estimates. Coupon collection failure retains valid flight listings and reports the limitation.
7. fare_quotes preserves directed route/date/adults/cabin/provider/verification/quality, departure time and stops. The reverse route is a different dataset. The brain receives current accepted rows, so a failed search cannot use yesterday's bargain to issue a new buy alert. History charts separate accepted listed fares and verified totals, honoring time/stops preferences; legacy rows without required scope evidence are excluded. Forecasting uses listed fares only. History/intelligence responses carry a per-request ID; changing criteria clears old output and ignores late responses for another query. The intelligence page uses the shared brain rather than a second legacy deadline verdict.
8. Round trips are separate outbound/inbound one-way searches. No combined-return cart or return-level coupon is implemented. Flexible-date listings are actual Google searches; the broader Explore action requires corroborated child searches for savings suggestions.

Coupon harvesting reads explicit published codes from live provider DOM, EMT's public flight/bank advertisement JSON feeds and best-effort public GrabOn merchant cards. Independent bounded collectors retain partial source failures. Current native terms take priority, then merchant feeds, then third-party cards; incomplete feed summaries stay eligibility-unconfirmed. Related merchant widgets are excluded. Provider codes and source terms are stored with dates/URLs; unknown expiry stays unknown. Old seeded offers are excluded unless newly observed or explicitly entered by the user. Failures retry after fifteen minutes rather than being cached as success for six hours. Advertised amounts never reduce a fare. EMI/BNPL, bank/card method/product, passenger minimums, app-only and child/infant-only offers are filtered. Optional extra card cashback is separated only when an independent immediate base discount remains; cashback-only conditions remain restricted. Conflicting domestic-only/international-only terms become unknown. New-user/traveller/network claims require selected evidence and otherwise stay conditional; fare-family, caps and exclusions still need provider validation. All eligible codes are attempted within the budget, with incomplete coverage disclosed. An optimizer run cannot replace an existing cheaper cart with a higher total: it fails and attempts original-code restoration.

Results include an after-search restriction box. Explicit “I don't have HDFC”, “exclude Flipkart Axis”, “only UPI”, “no EMI” and “no BNPL” update current constraints, exclude unavailable verified payments and recompute advice and the lowest corroborated listed/verified options across sites. Unrecognized requests produce an error. Changed preferences carry into the next search/Red Room request; Settings saves them across restarts. Advertised discounts are not subtracted to invent a next-best price.

## Persistence and monitoring

The normal store is backend/db/flight_agent.db. Old tables/data are preserved; active advice uses accepted fare_quotes. Explicit payment exclusions/financing settings persist locally in config/user_profile.json. Red Room saves directed route/date/adults/cabin/time/stops, financial preferences, optional total target, interval (6–168 hours), transfer estimate and optional exploration. There are at most ten active requests. Saved alerts deduplicate unchanged buy/wait signals. Errors retain the previous report but show a persisted latest-error warning. Removed requests cannot publish late recommendations. Older one-adult economy listed-fare watches remain supported separately; new users should use Red Room.

Enable-RedRoom.ps1 registers a limited-user Windows login task. red-room-background.ps1 waits for an existing backend or launches it hidden and lets Task Scheduler retry failures. This task was registered and observed running during validation. It does not run while the machine is off/asleep, and does not purchase tickets. Disable with ./Enable-RedRoom.ps1 -Disable. Desktop balloon delivery is best-effort; persistent alerts are authoritative. Optional browser alerts need user permission and an open tab.

## Sourced brain, forecast gate and alternatives

MarketContext refreshes at most every six hours: FRED Brent crude USD/barrel and USD/INR, EIA and PIB primary RSS, and separately labelled Google News reported headlines. Requests have timeouts. Feed failures retain dated cached data and failure status. Brent is a crude proxy, not India's aviation turbine fuel price. Headlines have publication times and source links; titles alone never prove a route price hike. Sources are indexed in local FTS5; this is an evidence store, not an active LLM/graph-RAG decision engine.

The active rules produce MONITOR, BUY_CANDIDATE (target met, observed bargain or departure deadline), or WAIT_AND_RECHECK (only a qualifying lower listed-fare forecast). They explain what triggered the result. Announced future offers are context only: their net benefit and full eligibility are not verified, so they cannot trigger waiting. Unannounced bank birthdays, years of seasonality, seat demand and future coupon releases are never assumed.

Daily minimums collapse repeated scrapes to one observation day. Listed fares and checkout totals are never mixed in a model. A robust recent slope can produce an exploratory seven-day estimate only after at least seven non-overlapping weekly holdouts beat a last-price baseline by 10% and the series is fresh. Gaps over seven days restart the forecast-only cohort after the latest gap while retaining older descriptive history; the latest completed validation outcome must be at most fourteen days old. These are conservative product policies, not learned universal thresholds. At least twenty weekly outcomes are required for a percentage, displayed as historical directional accuracy with a 95% Wilson interval. The interval assumes independent outcomes; nonoverlapping outcomes may remain correlated. It is not a probability that a news event causes a hike or a guarantee for this ticket. No collected real trip has enough days yet. The current certainty is “Not calibrated.” Historical data also lacks standardized baggage/fare-family/seat inventory and payment-condition controls; this limits any future inference.

Explore searches adjacent days, GOI/GOX airport substitution and up to two hubs (default BOM/AMD) using fresh Google/EaseMyTrip listings. Concurrency stays globally capped at three; a bounded cache lasts twenty minutes and includes time/stops criteria. Nonstop-only requests skip self-transfer hubs; changing that preference is an explicit user choice. GOI/GOX net comparison subtracts the entered ground cost, not an invented cab fare or road distance. Separate-ticket candidates require the same airport IATA, adults/cabin, consistent domestic arrival time, a four-hour minimum connection (five if terminals are known to differ), and at most twelve hours. Unknown terminals are labelled unknown; separate baggage/fees/protection remain unverified. A two-hour protected connection and a self-transfer are different risks. Fli rows include exact segments and published layover durations; browser rows without segment evidence cannot corroborate connecting itineraries for learning or savings claims.

Export only accepted evidence with backend/.venv/Scripts/python.exe backend/tools/export_brain_dataset.py. Output under ignored sessions/brain-data includes fares, feed caches, linked source records and offer snapshots. The current manifest records actual counts; repeated scrapes still cover only one observation day. Existing synthetic CSV/model files are preserved and inactive.

For isolated validation set FLIGHT_FINDER_DB and FLIGHT_FINDER_CONFIG_DIR to temporary locations. Sessions cannot survive backend restarts. Optional keys/proxies belong in ignored .env configuration, never source/docs. The app does not collect card information. Local screenshots/logs can contain provider page content and should be treated as local data.

## Follow-up repairs, 8 October 2026

The [follow-up validation report](../REPAIR_VALIDATION_2026-10-08.md) is the current status; the earlier validation remains a dated baseline. These changes were chosen to improve the reliability of evidence without a paid service or a dependency replacement that has not proved better.

`quote_contract.py` preserves the original listed price/time when a checkout is verified. Alternative comparisons use that listing snapshot or stop with an error. They do not substitute a payment total. The original listing must be less than twenty minutes old. Net savings stay unknown until applicable fees are verified; entered ground costs remain estimates. Journey-start time preferences apply to the first self-transfer leg, while total stops include both legs and the connection. Round-trip advice also compares two listing snapshots on the same basis.

`quote_integrity.py` now requires matching nonstop flight numbers or complete connecting segments, plus matching known fare-family/baggage evidence. Close price agreement means within the larger of INR 100 or 5% of the lower fare. Unknown fare conditions on both sites still limit comparability. Persisted accepted observations now carry `evidence_version=2`, source URL, segment/fare evidence, listing snapshot and integrity reasons. Older observations remain stored but are excluded from active history, forecasting and exports because their looser acceptance rules cannot be retrospectively certified.

`offer_rules.py` adds eligible/ineligible/unknown decisions and AND/OR/NOT predicates. It checks published booking validity, weekdays, minimum spend, account/redemption limits and payment restrictions. Bank/method alternatives and exclusions stay scoped to their combination. Unknown product/network/account/travel conditions are conditional, not evidence of eligibility. This is a deterministic parser for supported conditions, not complete understanding of arbitrary legal terms. Provider acceptance and a stable payable reduction are still required. The trial engine distinguishes accepted-without-saving, preserves known original-coupon terms and checks selected cart options, identity, payment and total during/after trials. A successful verification requires explicit directed airport codes, full date/year, flight number/time and adult/cabin evidence. Connecting checkout layouts without an exact provider segment reader remain unsupported. Later issuer/gateway charges are outside the merchant-displayed total's proof.

`provider_status.py` distinguishes access refusal, provider service failure and explicit no inventory. `provider_runs` stores exact criteria and failed-source missingness. Failure links open the exact manual search, and a failed booking browser remains available for assisted recovery. Diagnostics found Cleartrip itinerary creation HTTP 403 in Chromium and Edge, and no usable MakeMyTrip recovery in Chrome or with HTTP/2 disabled. No challenge bypass or protocol/browser workaround was adopted. Browser Use and Stagehand were considered as AI navigation helpers; neither was benchmarked as a superior replacement, and model/hosting costs would need separate assessment. Playwright remains the precise, free local browser controller.

The brain rejects stale/future/malformed current quotes and compares forecasts only with listing snapshots. Retrospective common-provider selection cannot establish leakage-free holdouts: calibration and percentile bargains are now withheld if provider coverage changes. The forecast/accuracy gate uses the same recent nonoverlapping weekly outcomes; explicit target dates clarify the seven-day horizon. Airline-specific history stays separate and pooled itinerary history cannot forecast a requested specific flight. It does not learn a fuel/news causal coefficient from headlines. No seasonal fare model or directed route-demand feed has been released. Cold-start certainty remains Not calibrated.

Real context bootstrap now gathers FRED Brent/USD-INR from January 2023, incrementally refreshing an overlapping recent window, and validates eight discovered January–August 2026 AAI passenger PDFs across seventeen airports. AAI counts arrivals plus departures; it is airport context, not directed route demand, unique travellers or seat inventory. Monthly/current and same-month prior-year columns are checked against printed percentages and kept separate from international/cumulative totals. The UI shows the actual month, prior-year comparator, units, source and staleness.

The reviewed IOCL historical table supplies 96 city-month INR/kilolitre observations from April 2024 through March 2026. It is explicitly stale, and ordinary HTTP/browser challenge responses are reported as unavailable. An optional reviewed-primary-text import does not pretend current scraping succeeded or replace old observation dates with an import/footer date. `backend/tools/bootstrap_brain_data.py` backs up SQLite and stores manifests in ignored sessions/source-bootstrap; source URLs, parser revision and content hashes describe accepted feed documents. Fuel/FX and traffic evidence never becomes fare training rows. See [AGENT_AUDIT_2026-10-08.md](../AGENT_AUDIT_2026-10-08.md) for counts, dates and validation.

Domestic country validation uses a dated, hashed public-domain OurAirports snapshot in backend/data/indian_airports.json instead of the old inaccurate handwritten list. Its 164 India IATA airport entries are a location reference, not proof of available commercial service. The existing short frontend airport menu remains a subset. A missing service returns unavailable inventory.

Red Room claims work with a fifteen-minute SQLite lease, bounds a complete check to ten minutes, releases leases on cancellation, separates last attempt from last success, and retries transient failures sooner. Persistent unique notification keys prevent duplicate saved/delivered alerts across processes. Desktop-delivery failure leaves the saved alert intact. The Windows startup script adds StartWhenAvailable; it still needs a logged-in, awake computer. Old illustrative airport ground costs are no longer seeded at startup; active planning uses real fares and the user's entered estimate. `FLIGHT_FINDER_PORT` supports an isolated local server (default 8765); the live validation tool accepts a matching `--url`.
