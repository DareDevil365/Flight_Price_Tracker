The product compares listed flight fares and helps verify the actual amount payable after coupons and checkout charges. A listed fare is never presented as a verified final price. There is no upfront card-ownership form: show conditional deals, then accept explicit exclusions in the results text box and compare remaining options across providers. Actual bank/product eligibility must match the selected payment method when verifying checkout. Future rewards do not reduce the payable amount.

The active application uses `backend/search_engine.py`, `backend/quote_integrity.py`, `backend/intelligence.py`, `backend/red_room.py`, `backend/alternative_planner.py`, `backend/booking_advisor.py` and `backend/travel_advisor.py`. The old dispatcher, synthetic-data predictor, RAG wiki and reusable research modules are retained for reference; they do not make live booking decisions.

New observations must match the directed route/date/adults/cabin, departure-time and stop constraints, and be corroborated across comparable provider listings or verified at checkout before they enter learning. BOM–HYD and HYD–BOM remain separate. Connecting itinerary corroboration requires exact segment evidence. Flagged prices are quarantined. Forecasting uses listed market fares; checkout totals are separate observations affected by payment/add-on choices. Confidence percentages require measured independent weekly outcomes and a passed model gate; cold starts say “Not calibrated.” Published news and offers retain links/dates and never create prices or certain future discounts.

Playwright remains the booking/browser engine. Only the tested public Google listing reader from Fli was integrated, pinned to an exact revision in backend/requirements-google.txt. It runs in a bounded child process and falls back to the Google browser worker. Its limitations and comparison results are in the validation report; no claim of universal superiority is made.

Red Room saves requests and checks them while the backend runs. Optional Windows login startup is implemented with `Enable-RedRoom.ps1`; a real GOI–HYD request for 15 December 2026 was saved during validation. Monitoring pauses when the computer is off/asleep. Nearby dates, GOI/GOX and separate-ticket comparisons use additional real searches, with unknown transfer costs/terminals labelled explicitly.

The app currently has adapters for Google Flights, EaseMyTrip, Cleartrip and MakeMyTrip. Website access varies, and the MakeMyTrip live check did not return usable listings. Other travel websites are manual comparison links. Universal coverage and a validated future-price forecast remain unfinished; see the limitations document.

The 8 October 2026 [zero-cost repair plan](ZERO_COST_REPAIR_PLAN.md) is partially implemented; its remaining proposals are not working features. The [follow-up report](REPAIR_VALIDATION_2026-10-08.md) distinguishes repairs from remote access failures. New code preserves listing snapshots, tightens itinerary corroboration, evaluates coupon conditions with eligible/ineligible/unknown outcomes, records provider failures, and adds database leases and persistent notification deduplication. It retains local SQLite and Playwright, keeps Gemini optional and requires no paid service.

New accepted history has evidence_version=2. Older observations remain stored but are excluded from active history, forecasting and exports because they were accepted under less strict rules. This is intentional: retaining more rows must not outweigh evidence quality. Checkout requires explicit directed airports, full date/year, flight identity, adult/cabin and selected payment evidence. Any unsupported layout remains unverified. The merchant-displayed payment total cannot establish whether a later gateway or issuer adds a charge.

The current scope is domestic Indian flight prices. Global Brent, USD/INR and disruption news remain relevant context. A sourced India airport reference replaces the inaccurate legacy country list; country membership does not prove current flight service.

The later 9 October [three-agent audit](AUDIT_AND_DATA_2026-10-09.md) supersedes that test count: **344 isolated backend tests**, frontend build/lint, four new complete app searches and live brain error/budget/reverse-route checks passed. It fixes missed EMT fares, deposit/hidden-price parsing, cabin/timezone/group-total checks, merchant-tab loss, malformed totals and conditional insurance-combo coupons. Advice now uses one decision consistently, respects missed targets and prioritizes a qualified declining trend over an unvalidated bargain. Partial inventory and context failures are visible; asynchronous brain failures return explicit trip-scoped errors.

Real startup context now includes 96 validated monthly IndiGo domestic capacity/utilisation rows and 100 dated IOCL city observations, alongside FRED and AAI. `backend/carrier_context.py` validates fiscal-year columns, units, published dates and RPK/ASK ratios; missing rows stay missing. Carrier-wide capacity cannot stand in for route demand, fare history or available seats. The newest ATF and carrier observations remain explicitly stale. The final accepted-fare export has 126 rows over thirteen exact cohorts, still one observation day each. Playwright and local SQLite were retained because the audited evidence boundaries matter more than replacing their engines. Final-payment coverage, exhaustive provider inventory and calibrated future-fare accuracy remain unproven.

The later 9 October page-inspection work uses `backend/page_inspection.py` to read rendered labels, coupon controls and INR monetary rows locally. Optional cropped screenshots stay in RAM and are cleared after inspection; page dumps were removed from the public probe tools. Playwright and SQLite remain because they already support this free, deterministic workflow. `backend/coupon_audit.py` adds a visible scoped trial ledger instead of treating a review acceptance as global coupon reliability. Unknown conditions stay conditional, sign-in overlays stop trials, and only a successfully restored, unchanged payment cart can be checkout-verified. Final validation passed 374 isolated tests plus frontend build/lint; four real coupon runs retain 152 trial records. MakeMyTrip homepage navigation worked in the in-app browser but failed in the app browser, so its candidate recovery is diagnostic-only. Cleartrip continuation and live final-payment coverage remain unresolved. See the new report for evidence, setup commands and tradeoffs.
